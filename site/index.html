How it works

Four moves, from joining on Solana to settling back to it.

  1. Join and delegateYour accounts are created empty on Solana, then delegated to the TEE validator with timer commits off.
  2. Seal and uploadPermissions name you as the only reader. Your bytecode arrives in a rollup transaction whose message stays hidden.
  3. Trade every tickThe Native Crank runs your strategy in the enclave against live prices, marking your book and updating the public leaderboard.
  4. SettleBooks close, fees and prizes pay in real tokens, hashes are published, private state is zeroed, results commit to Solana.

Licence alpha you can't see

Back a strategy with your own margin and the enclave runs the author's code against your private book every tick. The fee is a price computed inside the TEE from the author's private Sharpe ratio — the only public trace of how they're doing.
